To my sweetest girl

My Little Princess, I wish I could put into words just how much you mean to me. Sometimes I look at you and wonder how I got so lucky to have someone like you in my life. You make my days softer, my smiles bigger, and my world a little more beautiful just by being in it. I love you for the big things, of course, but I also love you for all the tiny things you might not even notice. The way you talk, the way you laugh, your little habits, your silly moments, your quiet moments, and all the things that make you wonderfully, unmistakably you. I made this because I never want you to wonder whether you are special to me. You are. Not because you have to be perfect, not because you have to make every day magical, but simply because you are you. You deserve tenderness, patience, reassurance, laughter, and a love that keeps showing up. So here are one hundred reasons why I love you. Some are sweet, some are silly, and some are the little things I carry in my heart. And even after all one hundred, I know I will keep finding more.
With all my love, your boy ❤️

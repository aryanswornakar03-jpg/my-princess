My sweetest Princess,

I wish I could be right beside you while you read this, holding your hands and looking into your eyes. But until I can do that, I hope these words make you feel even a little of the love I carry for you.

There is one thing I want you to know, and I never want you to doubt it: I am not planning a life where I simply walk away from you. I want to keep choosing you, keep learning you, keep growing with you, and keep finding reasons to fall in love with you all over again.

I know life won't always be perfect. We may have difficult days, misunderstandings, distance, and moments when things feel hard. But I don't want us to give up on each other just because a day is difficult. I want us to talk, understand, forgive, and face those days together. I want to be someone who makes you feel safe, heard, respected, and loved.

And one day, my love, I want to stand in front of you, hold your hands, and ask you to be mine forever. I want to marry you. Not just as a sweet dream or a pretty sentence, but as a future I genuinely hope and intend to build with you—one honest step at a time. 💍

I can't promise that every day will be easy, but I can promise to keep putting love, effort, patience, and honesty into us. I want to celebrate your happiest moments, comfort you on your hardest days, and make ordinary little moments beautiful simply because I get to share them with you.

So whenever you feel unsure, come back to this letter and remember: you are not someone I want only for a little while. You are the person I hope to build a home, a thousand memories, and a lifetime of tomorrows with.

Thank you for being my princess, my favourite person, and such a beautiful part of my life. I love you more than these words can ever explain.

Forever hoping for our forever,
Your boy ♡
My future wife. 💍

I hope one day I get to read this beside you, in the home we built together. Until then, let's keep choosing each other. I love you, Princess. ♡

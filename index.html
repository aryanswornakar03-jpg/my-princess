My Dearest Princess,

My little princess, my cutie pie, my favourite person… ❤️

I wish I could be beside you right now, holding your hands, looking into your eyes, and telling you just how much you mean to me. But until the day I can do that, I wanted to create this little place where you can always come and feel how deeply you are loved.

You are so much more than just someone I love. You are someone I want to share my ordinary days with. Someone I want to laugh with over the smallest things, talk to about absolutely nothing, and make beautiful memories with.

I never want you to question whether you are special to me. You are. More than words could ever properly explain. I feel incredibly lucky to have you in my life.

And my princess, I have a dream. One day, I want to stand beside you, hold your hand, and call you my wife. I want to build a home with you, celebrate our little victories, comfort each other through hard days, and grow old with a million memories behind us.

Until that day comes, let me love you through every message, every call, every little effort, and every tomorrow.

You're my favourite chapter, and I hope our story has a thousand beautiful chapters left to write.

Forever yours ♡
